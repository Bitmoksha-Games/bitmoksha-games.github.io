Privacy Policy

Minefield Mayhem

Last Updated: 14/01/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Minefield Mayhem(“we”, “our”, or “the game”) handles user information. We are committed to protecting player privacy and being transparent about how data is handled during gameplay and development.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide

Minefield Mayhem does not require players to create an account or submit personal information such as name, email address, or phone number.

b) Automatically Collected Information

At the current internal testing stage, the game does not integrate analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or third-party backend services that collect personal data.

The game may collect limited non-personal, device-level information required for basic functionality, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version

This information is not used to identify individual users.

2. How We Use Information

Any information collected is used solely to:

  • Ensure the game functions correctly
  • Improve gameplay experience and stability
  • Identify and fix bugs during testing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

3. Multiplayer & Online Features

Minefield Mayhem includes online multiplayer gameplay. During internal testing, multiplayer features do not collect or store personal user information.

In future versions, backend services may be introduced to support matchmaking, progression, or competitive features. If this changes, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Third-Party Services

The game currently does not use third-party services such as:

  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics)
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting tools

If such services are added in future builds, this Privacy Policy will be updated to clearly disclose what data is collected and how it is used.

5. Ads and In-App Purchases

Advertisements are not enabled in the current internal test build.
Any future monetization features, including ads or in-app purchases, will be implemented responsibly and disclosed through an updated Privacy Policy.

6. Children’s Privacy

Minefield Mayhem is designed to be suitable for a general audience, including users under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not require account creation or the submission of personal data.

If future versions introduce features that involve data collection or online services, we will ensure compliance with applicable child data protection laws, including COPPA and Google Play Families policies, and update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has shared personal information may contact us, and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Security practices will continue to evolve as the game develops and new systems are introduced.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the game evolves. Any changes will be reflected in this document with an updated revision date.
