1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy covers Minesweeper360 (the “Game”), provided by BITMOKSHA GAMES (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED, trading as BitMoksha Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It explains the handling of information through the Game and our support communications.

The Game is available on Google Play under the package identifier com.bitmoksha.minefieldmayhempvp. The package identifier remains the same despite the change of the Game’s display name to Minesweeper360.

We do not operate our own server for player profiles, scores, or saved progress. Third-party services integrated into the Game can still process information on their systems.

2. Information handled

Information processed by integrated services

The Game uses advertising and analytics tools. Depending on their versions, configuration, available device permissions, and consent status, information processed by those tools can include:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version, language, and technical information about the installation.
  • Identifiers: an app-instance identifier, advertising identifier where available and enabled, and other identifiers used by the relevant service.
  • Usage and advertising activity: app opens, session activity, gameplay events configured for analytics, ad impressions, ad interactions, and rewarded-ad events.
  • Network and diagnostic information: IP address and information about SDK operation and performance. An IP address can be used to infer an approximate location.

These categories describe potential processing by the listed integrations; they do not mean every tool collects every category. Identifiers and device information may be personal information even when they do not contain a name.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include, such as a support question or an attached screenshot. Please avoid sending sensitive information or unnecessary information about a child.

Information on your device

Local game data, such as preferences or progress saved by the Game, remains on your device unless handled by a connected feature or your device’s backup service. Your operating system or backup provider controls its own backup practices.

3. How information is used

Information described in this policy is used to support these purposes:

  • Run the Game and maintain its settings and supported features.
  • Display and measure advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements.
  • Understand app usage and improve the playing experience.
  • Identify technical problems, prevent invalid advertising activity, and protect service integrity.
  • Respond to support enquiries, privacy requests, and legal obligations.

Advertising personalization and analytics processing depend on the integrated services’ settings and applicable privacy requirements. This policy is a notice about information handling; it does not replace a consent request where consent is required.

5. Information sharing

Integrated services may receive information directly from the Game and process it for the purposes described above. Information sent directly to a provider does not need to pass through a server operated by us.

Support information may be handled through our email service. We may disclose information we hold when required by law, to address fraud or security incidents, or to protect legal rights. We limit such disclosures to the information relevant to the purpose.

This policy does not characterize all advertising or analytics data as anonymous. The providers’ policies explain their processing responsibilities and any independent uses of information.

6. Children’s privacy

Minesweeper360 has a mixed audience that includes children aged three and above. A store age rating does not itself provide parental consent for information collection.

The Game currently applies the same advertising and analytics settings to all players. Consequently, the service-related processing described in this policy may also apply to children. This draft does not establish that personalized advertising is disabled, that identifiers are restricted, or that any required parental consent mechanism has been implemented.

A parent or guardian may contact us to ask about information relating to their child, request access or deletion where applicable, or ask us to stop further collection. We may need limited information to verify the request and locate relevant records. We will address requests concerning information under our control and explain when action through a third-party provider is also needed.

Contact contact@bitmoksha.com with the subject “Minesweeper360 child privacy request.” Please do not include a child’s full name, birth date, or other sensitive details in the initial message.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Device advertising controls: Android provides advertising privacy controls, including the ability to reset or delete an advertising ID on supported versions. Availability and menu names vary. These controls do not stop all advertising or all analytics collection.
  • Consent choices: where the Game displays a consent or privacy-options interface, use it to review the choices offered. Device settings and a privacy policy do not replace consent where legally required.
  • Stop using the Game: uninstalling stops future collection by that installation, but does not automatically delete information already processed by us or providers.
  • Contact us: depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent. You may also have a right to complain to a privacy regulator.

Send requests to contact@bitmoksha.com. We may request proportionate verification and will respond within the period required by applicable law. Some requests may require assistance from Google or another provider, particularly for information we cannot identify or access directly.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the enquiry, manage related requests, and meet applicable legal requirements. We do not maintain a developer-operated server containing player profiles, scores, or saved progress.

Advertising and analytics information is retained according to the relevant service’s retention rules and the settings applied to that service. Uninstalling the Game does not necessarily remove those records. Contact us to request deletion of information under our control or help identifying the relevant provider’s privacy options.

You can use Android’s app-storage controls to remove local app data. This can erase saved preferences or progress. Device backups are managed separately by your backup provider.

9. Security and international processing

Information handled through Google services is subject to the security measures described in Google’s documentation. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage guarantees complete security.

We are based in India. Third-party providers may process information in other countries under their service terms and transfer arrangements. Those countries may have privacy rules that differ from the rules where you live. See the providers’ privacy notices for their international processing practices.

10. External links

Advertisements or links in the Game may take you to services operated by others. Their privacy notices apply to activity on those services. Review their practices before providing information.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Game, its services, or applicable requirements. The published policy will show its effective or last-updated date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before a material change takes effect.
